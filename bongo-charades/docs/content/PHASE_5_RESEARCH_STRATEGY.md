# Bongo Charades — Phase 5 Research Strategy (Stage 2)

**Status:** APPROVED by the owner (2026-11-13, "as recommended"). Delivered in Stages 3–7; see `PHASE_5_RESEARCH_REPORT.md` and `PHASE_5_CONTENT_AUDIT.md`.
**Date:** 2026-11-13 · **Branch:** `phase-5-content`
**Goal set by the owner:** every one of the 29 categories gets **100 or more well-researched prompts** (today: 12–32 each, 489 ideas in total).

---

## 1. What "well researched" will mean

Every prompt must pass three separate questions. A prompt that fails any one is left out.

| Question | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Popular / documented?** | There is evidence it exists and matters (a chart, an article, an award list, a reference work). |
| **Familiar?** | Ordinary players in the target audience would recognise it, not only fans or critics. |
| **Playable?** | It can be acted out, described or hummed in a few seconds, in 4 words or fewer. |

A song at number 1 on a chart is *popular*. That does not make it *familiar* to a mixed group, or *playable*.

### Confidence levels (recorded for every researched prompt)
- **High** — two independent sources, or one authoritative source (official chart, award record, reference work).
- **Medium** — one editorial source, or strong general knowledge with no dated source.
- **Needs local verification** — I found some evidence but cannot confirm how people really use or recognise it. These prompts get `needsReview: true` and go on the owner's review list. They are never presented as confirmed.

### What does not need a source per prompt
Universal Classic content (a lion, a toothbrush, "brushing teeth") is common knowledge. For these, the ledger records the category-level method (for example "countries taken from the UN member list") rather than a source for each word.

### Rules that never bend
- No invented chart positions, stream counts, dates, achievements or slang meanings.
- No lyrics or script lines. No defamatory or unkind prompts about real people. Party-appropriate only.
- Every source is logged with: topic, source name, URL, publication date, date accessed, region, what it proves, confidence, limits.

3. **Chart snapshots date quickly.** Each one is logged with its date. Trending prompts get a review date.
4. Some sites may block me part-way through. Where a planned source fails, the ledger will say so rather than silently substituting memory.

## 5. Keeping categories apart at this size

The bank already forbids the same text in two categories. With six times more content the boundaries need to be explicit:

| Possible overlap | Rule |
|---|---|
| Tanzanian artist or song | Bongo Flava (current, or about 2012 onward) or Zilipendwa (older). Never in African Music & Artists, Throwback Hits or Music & Artists. |
| Tanzanian celebrity who is a musician | Bongo Flava, not Watu Maarufu. |
| African song before about 2012 | Throwback Hits. After: African Music & Artists. |
| Global song or artist | Music & Artists (current or timeless) or Throwback Hits (clearly a throwback). Never both. |
| Global film, series, celebrity | Movies & TV for titles and characters; Pop Culture for trends, memes, celebrities. |
| Tanzanian dish | Chakula Chetu only. Food & Drinks stays international. |
| Bodaboda, bajaji, daladala | Daladala Diaries for transport moments; Mtaani for street life generally. |
| Dar es Salaam places | Hii Dar Yetu only. Other Tanzanian towns may appear in African Cities if not used elsewhere. |
| Car makers | Car Brands only; never Famous Brands. |

Where a reference truly belongs in two places (for example "Malaika", whose origin is disputed between Tanzania and Kenya), the content audit will state the choice and the reason.

## 6. Proposed prompt structure (to be built in Stage 5, shown here for approval)

Only fields the game needs are shipped inside the app. Research detail stays in the ledger.

**In the app (each prompt):**
| Field | Values | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | Stable, made from the content (for example `anim-lion-en`) | Remembering played prompts across sessions. Today's ids are position numbers and would shift when prompts are added. |
| `text`, `categoryId`, `language`, `concept`, `hint`, `needsReview` | Unchanged | As today. |
| `difficulty` | `easy` · `medium` · `hard` | Balanced dealing. Hard means harder to act, not more obscure. |
| `kind` | `person` · `song` · `place` · `thing` · `action` · `phrase` · `title` · `brand` · `food` | Avoiding five artists in a row. |
| `era` (optional) | `1970s` … `2020s` | Avoiding several prompts from one decade in a row. Music and nostalgia only. |
| `shelf` | `evergreen` · `trending` · `nostalgia` | Knowing what to re-check over time. |

**In the research ledger only (not shipped):** sources, confidence, region or scope, date last reviewed, notes.

**Files:**
- `docs/content/research/SOURCES.md` — the source registry (one numbered entry per source).
- `docs/content/research/<group>-<category>.md` — evidence per category, each prompt pointing to source numbers.
- `scripts/content/build_prompts.py` stays the single editable source of the prompt bank.

## 8. Keeping content current (outline; detailed in Stage 7)

- **Trending** prompts carry a review date. Proposed check: every 3 months, against the same chart sources.
- **Evergreen** and **nostalgia** prompts are reviewed once a year.
- Changes are proposed as a list, approved by the owner, then released as a numbered content version.
- The app never calls a live chart service. All content ships inside the app.
