## 2. African Music & Artists

### 2.1 The main finding: charts are local
I compared this week's YouTube top 100 for Tanzania, Kenya, Uganda, Nigeria, South Africa and Zimbabwe [S1, S36].

- Only **two** tracks are on all six: "Heaven Baby" (Ayra Starr ft. Zayn) and "Dai Dai" (Shakira).
- "Slick" (Victony) is on five. "Rush" (Ayra Starr) and "Bandana" (Fireboy DML) are on four.
- South Africa's and Zimbabwe's charts share almost nothing with East or West Africa.
- Uganda's chart is mostly Luganda-language artists (King Saha, Spice Diana, Sheebah, John Blaq).

**What this means:** a song at number one in Lagos or Johannesburg is often unknown in Dar es Salaam. Country charts prove *popular*, not *familiar* to this game's players. For familiarity I will lean on three things instead: awards and international crossovers, long-running songs, and **African songs that chart inside Tanzania**.

## 5. African Cities

The list of the 100 largest cities [S44] confirms names and countries. Players will know the big capitals and a few famous others; they will not know most second cities (Mbuji-Mayi, Nnewi, Abomey-Calavi). A realistic set is the 54 capitals plus about 25–30 famous non-capitals (Lagos, Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, Casablanca, Marrakesh, Alexandria, Mombasa, Kumasi, Goma, Lubumbashi), plus Tanzanian towns not used elsewhere (Mwanza, Arusha, Dodoma, Mbeya, Tanga).

**Honest estimate:** 80–90 recognisable cities. Reaching 100 means including capitals few people can name (Moroni, Praia, Malabo), which would be rated hard.

## 8. Decisions needed from the owner before curation

1. **Throwback cut-off.** Use the same year as Zilipendwa: 2013 or earlier is Throwback Hits, 2014 onward is African Music & Artists? (Recommended: yes. One rule is easier to remember and it keeps "Oliver Twist" and "Chop My Money" as throwbacks.)
2. **North Africa's share.** Lower the floor from 10% to about 5%? (Recommended: yes. The charts show almost no crossover, and many titles are in Arabic script, which fails the "playable" test.)
3. **Musicians in African Icons.** Move "Fela Kuti" and "Miriam Makeba" into the music categories, as we did for Tanzanian musicians? (Recommended: yes, for one consistent rule.)
4. **Sitting political leaders.** Leave current heads of state out of African Icons and keep to historical figures and non-political people? (Recommended: yes.)
5. **Sheng and other non-Tanzanian slang.** Allow up to about ten of the best-known words, all on your tick-list, or none? (Recommended: up to ten.)
