### 2.5 Findings that affect curation
1. **The current bank stops at about 2022.** None of the 2025 top ten is in the game. This confirms the gap named in the handoff.
2. **Gospel is a large share of what Tanzania plays.** The YouTube top 100 includes Joel Lwaga, Neema Gospel Choir, Kabi Catholic, Rehema Simfukwe, Japhet Zabron, Bahati Bukuku and others. Gospel is not Bongo Flava. **Owner decision needed:** leave gospel out, or allow a small number of very well-known gospel songs?
3. **Singeli is clearly part of the picture** (its own award categories; Dulla Makabila and D Voice in the chart). It fits inside Bongo Flava as "music culture".
4. **Foreign songs chart in Tanzania too** (amapiano, Nigerian pop, Bien from Kenya). These belong in African Music & Artists, not here.
5. **Name clashes to resolve**, because the bank forbids the same text twice:
   - "Malaika" — Mbosso's current hit versus the classic already in Throwback Hits.
   - "Salome" — Dully Sykes (2000s) versus Diamond Platnumz (2016).
   - "Zilipendwa" — also the title of a WCB Wasafi song.
   - "Nakshi Nakshi" (Lava Lava, 2026) versus "Nakshi Mrembo" (Ali Kiba).
   The artist hint line can tell them apart, but only one of each pair can be used.
6. **Song titles that are everyday words** ("Watu", "Wewe", "DJ", "Furaha") are easy to read but hard to guess as songs. The artist hint will carry these; many will rate `hard`.

**Evidence-backed candidates so far:** roughly 150 songs and 50 artists. The 100+ target is reachable on evidence.

## 4. Mtaani

**Main source:** the *Lugha ya Mitaani* dictionary [S19], 1,100 words collected 2000–2006, grouped by topic. Examples it documents:

| Topic | Words recorded |
|---|---|
| Friends | msela / masela, mshikaji, mchizi, braza, broo, mshirika |
| Money | mkwanja, mshiko, mapene, chapaa, buku, mavumba, faranga |
| Work and deals | dili, mchongo, misheni, deiwaka, kupiga debe, kubangaiza |
| Places | kitaani, geto, kijiweni, skani, majuu, Bongo |
| Greetings | "Mambo vipi?", "Poa", "Fiti", "Safi", "Baridi" |

**Limits and how they will be handled**
1. **The source is about 20 years old.** Some words are now ordinary Swahili, some are gone, and some have changed meaning. The research can show a word *was* in use; it cannot show it *is*. Every Mtaani word will go on the owner's tick-list with three choices: still used · old-fashioned · not known.
2. **Nothing documents current youth slang.** My search in Swahili found nothing usable, and social media is closed to me. The three slang phrases already flagged in the game (Kupiga Pamba, Utajua Hujui, Haina Kufeli) remain unverified.
3. **The dictionary includes crude and sexual words.** Those are excluded.
4. **Where a word is used.** The study centres on Dar es Salaam. Each word will be marked: national · Dar · youth · dated.
5. Research datasets from the University of Dar es Salaam [S20] exist but have not been opened yet.

Besides slang, the category also holds street jobs, places and habits (machinga, mama ntilie, kinyozi, bao, drafti). These are common knowledge and will be recorded as **Medium**.

**Evidence-backed candidates so far:** roughly 60 documented words, all needing the "still used?" check.
