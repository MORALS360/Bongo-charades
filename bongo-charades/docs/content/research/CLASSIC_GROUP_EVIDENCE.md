# Phase 5, Stage 3C — Research Evidence: Classic Charades

**Status:** research record for owner review. **No prompts have been written or added to the game.**
**Date:** 2026-11-14 · Sources are numbered in [`SOURCES.md`](SOURCES.md) (S49–S57).

Classic Charades is universal, everyday content. As agreed in Stage 2, most of it does not need a source per prompt: a lion, a toothbrush or "brushing teeth" is common knowledge. Research here checks two things instead: which **famous titles, people, places and brands** are truly the best known, and where the **Tanzanian player** differs from a global one.

## 2. Findings per category

### Countries [S49]
193 UN members. Reaching 100 is easy; making them all guessable is not. Curation should take every African country first, then the largest and best-known elsewhere, and rate small island states hard. The game already has Swahili names for many countries (Ujerumani, Ufaransa); pairs are worth extending.

### Famous Landmarks [S50]
- **Wonders:** the New 7 Wonders and the Seven Ancient Wonders give 14 world-famous names, most already in the game.
- **African UNESCO sites:** Kilimanjaro, Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Stone Town, Kilwa Kisiwani, Victoria Falls, Robben Island, Lalibela, Timbuktu, Djenné, Great Zimbabwe, Okavango Delta, Gorée, Aksum, Lamu, Bwindi, Virunga, Rwenzori.
- **Problem:** several of these are already candidates in other categories (Lalibela, Bwindi, Virunga, Lamu in East African Vibes; Stone Town in African Cities). See decision 2.
- My estimate holds at **80–100** well-known landmarks.

### Movies & TV [S52]
- **Top box office:** Avengers: Endgame, Avatar, Titanic, Star Wars, Spider-Man, Jurassic World, The Lion King, Frozen, Fast & Furious, Black Panther, Harry Potter, Barbie, Toy Story, Minions, Inside Out, Zootopia, Moana, Shrek, Finding Nemo, Ice Age, Despicable Me.
- **Netflix's most watched:** Squid Game, Wednesday, Stranger Things, Adolescence, KPop Demon Hunters, Red Notice, Bird Box.
- **The gap:** these lists show global fame. Tanzanian viewers also watch Indian films, Korean dramas, Turkish series and telenovelas on local channels; I found no source on this. Curation will add a few well-known ones from general knowledge, marked Medium.

### Music & Artists [S53]
- **Most-streamed artists:** Bruno Mars, The Weeknd, Rihanna, Justin Bieber, Lady Gaga, Taylor Swift, Drake, Ariana Grande, Michael Jackson, Coldplay, Shakira, Ed Sheeran, Billie Eilish, Eminem, Beyoncé, Chris Brown and others.
- **Most-streamed songs:** Blinding Lights, Shape of You, Starboy, As It Was, One Dance (Drake ft. **Wizkid**), Someone You Loved, Perfect, Die With a Smile, Believer, Dance Monkey.
- **2025 U.S. hits:** Die With a Smile, Luther, Birds of a Feather, APT., Espresso, Not Like Us, Golden (from KPop Demon Hunters).
- **Boundary:** global songs from 2013 or earlier are already Throwback Hits candidates; Music & Artists gets artists, instruments, genres, dance moves, and songs from 2014 onward.

### Sports [S54, S55]
- Olympic sports give the full list of sports and events.
- **Famous athletes** (Forbes 2025–26, Ballon d'Or): Cristiano Ronaldo, Lionel Messi, LeBron James, Stephen Curry, Lewis Hamilton, Canelo Álvarez, Tyson Fury, Karim Benzema, Shohei Ohtani; plus well-known names such as Kylian Mbappé, Erling Haaland, Neymar, Usain Bolt, Serena Williams, Mike Tyson.
- **In Tanzania,** football is clearly first; boxing and netball follow in some sources. No survey was found.
- **Boundary:** African athletes are African Icons candidates; this list should take global ones.

### Famous Brands [S51]
- All three rankings agree on a core: Apple, Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Samsung, Coca-Cola, McDonald's, Nike, Disney, Netflix, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Louis Vuitton, Chanel, Visa, Toyota, Mercedes-Benz, BMW.
- **Car makers** in these lists belong to Car Brands (owner rule).
- Many top-value brands (banks, business software, Chinese state companies) are **not** familiar to players and will be left out.
- **Question:** the rankings leave out brands every Tanzanian knows but that are not global (mobile networks, local drinks, Azam). See decision 1.

### Car Brands [S56] (summary only)
- **Toyota dominates Tanzania.** About a quarter of new registrations, and the main car of most car-owning households; most cars on the road are used Japanese imports.
- Next most visible: Suzuki, Isuzu, Nissan, Ford, Ashok Leyland; Chery grew strongly in 2025.
- **What this means:** Japanese brands (Toyota, Nissan, Subaru, Mitsubishi, Mazda, Suzuki, Isuzu, Honda) are the most familiar; truck and bus brands are visible on Tanzanian roads; Chinese brands are rising. See decision 3.
- The approved target stays **about 50–60**.

### Animals, Everyday Things, Act It Out, Food & Drinks
Common knowledge; no per-item source. Curation will:
- give every item an English–Kiswahili pair, as today;
- keep **Food & Drinks international** (no Tanzanian dishes, which belong to Chakula Chetu);
- avoid repeating Bongo-group actions (for example "Kusukuma Chapati" in Chakula Chetu);
- check Kiswahili names for less common animals against a dictionary in curation.

## 3. Decisions needed from the owner before curation

1. **Local brands in Famous Brands.** Allow brands every Tanzanian knows but that are not global (Vodacom, Airtel, Azam, Kilimanjaro Lager, Safari Lager, CRDB, NMB), up to about 15 of the 100? (Recommended: yes. Brands are about recognition, and these are more familiar to players than many global ones.)
2. **African landmarks.** Keep each place in one category only: world-famous landmarks (Kilimanjaro, Victoria Falls, Pyramids, Serengeti, Robben Island) in Famous Landmarks; places already chosen for East African Vibes or African Cities stay there. (Recommended: yes.)
3. **Car Brands scope.** Include truck and bus brands seen on Tanzanian roads (Scania, Ashok Leyland, Tata, Fuso, Yutong) and the rising Chinese brands (Chery, BYD, Geely), still brands only? (Recommended: yes. This brings the list to about 60–70 well-known names without obscure ones.)
4. **Tanzanian TV habits in Movies & TV.** Allow a small number of foreign shows that are popular on Tanzanian channels (Indian, Korean, Turkish, Latin American), marked for your check? (Recommended: yes, up to about 10.)
