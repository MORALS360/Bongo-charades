## Boundary rules used
- **Songs only in Throwback Hits.** Artists of any era go to African Music & Artists (or, for global artists, Music & Artists in the Classic group).
- **International songs from 2013 or earlier are in Throwback Hits.** Stage 4C must not repeat them in Music & Artists.
- **African film and series titles are in Pop Culture; global titles stay in Movies & TV.** African actors are in African Icons.
- **Tanzanians are not in African Icons.** Tanzanian towns outside Dar es Salaam are in African Cities.
- **East African Vibes avoids things that are also Tanzanian** (ugali, chapati, bodaboda); those stay in the Bongo group.

## Long famous titles (owner rule, 2026-11-14)
The usual limit is four words. A **really famous** title may have up to six words; it is marked `famous=yes` in the list and shown in the generated file. Restored under this rule: "I Will Always Love You", "My Heart Will Go On", "I Want It That Way", "Who Let the Dogs Out", "The Gods Must Be Crazy", "Land of a Thousand Hills".

## Honest limits
- **Throwback Hits is the weakest list for evidence.** 79 of 166 are on the tick-list. For the African classics I recalled the song and artist from memory and could not check them against a source; the question for the owner is both "is this correct?" and "do Tanzanians know it?".
- **Familiarity in Tanzania is mostly unproven.** Sources show these things exist and matter somewhere in Africa. Only the songs that chart inside Tanzania have direct evidence of being known there.
- **African Cities has no tick-list items but many "hard" ones.** 36 of 105 are capitals or cities few players can name. They are correct, not necessarily fun.
- **Pop Culture dates fastest.** Trend words, creators and challenges are marked trending and need re-checking every few months.
- **Difficulty ratings are my estimates.** English wording has not been tested with players; Kiswahili pairs are few and unreviewed.
- **Not opened:** a chart for Central Africa, Apple Music year-end lists for other countries, Google's Year in Search, any editorial list of African classics.
