## Owner decisions applied (approved after Stage 3A)
1. **Gospel is left out** of Bongo Flava.
2. **2013 or earlier is Zilipendwa; 2014 onward is Bongo Flava.** "Mwana" and "Hawajui" move to Bongo Flava.
3. **Zilipendwa also holds bands, legendary artists and dance styles.** The current test that every Zilipendwa prompt has an artist hint will need relaxing in Stage 5.
4. **Thin categories rest on general knowledge**, marked Medium or sent to the owner's tick-list.

## What the confidence labels mean here
- **High:** an official chart, an award record, or two sources. Also prompts already in the game that a source backs.
- **Medium:** one editorial or reference source, **or my own general knowledge with no source** (shown as source `GK`). Most everyday-life prompts are in this group.
- **Needs local verification:** I could not confirm it. This covers all slang, names I recalled without a source, older songs I recalled without a source, and anything where suitability is a judgement call.

## Boundary rules used
- Musicians → Bongo Flava or Zilipendwa. So "Professor Jay" and "Lady Jaydee" move out of Watu Maarufu into Zilipendwa.
- Film and comic actors → Movie za Bongo. Stand-up, TV and online comedians → Watu Maarufu.
- Footballers, coaches and club officials → Mpira & Mashabiki. The three football names already in Watu Maarufu (Mbwana Samatta, Haji Manara, Ahmed Ally) stay where they are.
- Dar es Salaam places → Hii Dar Yetu. Commuting moments → Daladala Diaries. Other street life → Mtaani.
- The couple's story → Mapenzi & Mahusiano. The ceremonies and family gatherings → Shughuli za Familia.
- Sitting politicians other than the President are left out of Watu Maarufu.

## Songs that share a title with something else (owner rule, 2026-11-14)
The owner decided that a big song is **not** left out because its title matches another prompt. The artist line under the title tells them apart: the city shows "Nairobi" alone, the song shows "Nairobi" with "Marioo ft. Bien" beneath it.

Kept under this rule: "Nairobi", "Mombasa", "Malaika", "DJ", "Banana", "Phone", "Oya", "Wewe", "Zilipendwa" (the WCB Wasafi song), "Baridi" (two songs, Jay Melody and D Voice), "Salome" (two songs, Dully Sykes and Diamond Platnumz), "Mbagala" and "Mjomba". The generated curation file lists every shared title.

**What this means for Stage 5:** the game today has a test that forbids the same text twice. It will change to: the same text may appear twice only when at least one is a song and the artist hints differ. Two prompts with the same text and no hint stay forbidden.

## Long famous titles (owner rule, 2026-11-14)
The usual limit is four words. A **really famous** title may have up to six words; it is marked `famous=yes` in the list and shown in the generated file. Restored under this rule: "Tanzania Ndio Nchi ya Furaha", "Nyama ya Bata ni Tamu".

## Honest limits
- **Watu Maarufu has 81 candidates, below 100.** I stopped where I could no longer name people I was confident are widely known, non-musicians, and uncontroversial. This is the "may ship under 100 with the reason recorded" case approved in Stage 2.
- **Counts will fall after the owner's review.** 295 candidates are on the tick-lists. Mtaani (75), Movie za Bongo (46), Zilipendwa (43), Mpira (40) and Watu Maarufu (32) carry most of them. A category could drop below 100 if many are rejected.
- **Difficulty ratings are my estimates**, not tested with players.
- **Some song years are uncertain.** For "Nitakupwelepweta", "Mfalme", "Moyo Mashine" and "Nashindwa" I placed them in Bongo Flava from the 2012–2022 ranking without confirming the exact year; if any is from 2013 or earlier it belongs in Zilipendwa.
- **The Kiswahili has not been reviewed by a native speaker.** Wording and spelling of everyday prompts should be read through by the owner, not only the tick-list items.
- **Still not opened:** Boomplay's chart, the two University of Dar es Salaam slang datasets, and the "summary only" sources in the registry. No current slang was verified.
- **Trending songs date quickly.** The 2026 chart entries are one week's snapshot (week ending 5 November 2026).
